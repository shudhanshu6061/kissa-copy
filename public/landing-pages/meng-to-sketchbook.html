OUR STORY

Some beginnings happen quietly.

An offer letter. A key to a new city. A first customer. A morning when you simply decide to try again. These moments rarely arrive with the right words you only know that something has started to move. KISSA was made for exactly these moments. In Hindi, kissa* means a story. That is what we create: a story inspired perfume you can hold, read, and wear. Every KISSA begins as a real hardcover book pages you turn one by one, a short story that leads somewhere, and the fragrance revealed only at the end.

Our first chapter, NAYA PANNA, celebrates the moment when the whole road isn't clear yet but you're ready to take the first step.

Craftsmanship & Alchemy

The Botanical Atelier

Born between the ancient spice routes of Singapore and the copper stills of Grasse.

Every KISSA formulation begins with a living watercolor sketch and a specimen plate. We collect rare botanical absolutes—wild Vanda orchids, Java vetiver aged for eight seasons, and incense resin distilled at dawn.

Our perfumes are deliberately composed at Extrait de Parfum concentration (26% to 32% aromatic oils), allowing the oils to meld with the natural heat of human skin rather than evaporating rapidly on alcohol.

The flacon is a tribute to tactile permanence: heavy French fluted glass topped with an unlacquered, turned solid brass cap that develops a warm natural patina over time.

01

Cold Maceration

Each batch rests in dark glass demijohns for 90 days before filtration.
